About these estimations

Temperature: Elevation and geographic ridge regression, followed by inverse-distance interpolation, from station data.

Rainfall: Inverse-distance interpolation from station data.

Station Data:
2010–2025: Εθνικό Αστεροσκοπείο Αθηνών / Meteo.gr, Ελληνική Μετεωρολογική Υπηρεσία (ΕΜΥ), various other sources of trustworthy stations.
1961–1990: Ελληνική Μετεωρολογική Υπηρεσία (ΕΜΥ) and ERA5.

The 1961–1990 temperature estimate applies the ERA5 period difference to the 2010–2025 station interpolation. A fine grid is not a measurement at every point. Mountain exposure, urban effects and rainfall gradients can remain unresolved. Wind comes from the nearest supplied ERA5 grid point, whose coordinates and distance appear above.

Annual temperature and rainfall use the supplied annual station columns. Annual wind roses pool all valid hours. Land boundary: Natural Earth. Terrain: Mapzen Terrain Tiles / Skadi.
